How to Play: Kahoot Relay Race (5 Questions)

  1. 1 Join with your team (Groups 1–8) and wait in the lobby. All groups start simultaneously when Admin initiates the race.
  2. 2 Each group receives 5 sequential envelope questions. Break the seal to reveal your clue and puzzle fragments!
  3. 3 Collaborate with your group to arrange the pieces, or speak the answer via voice recognition.
  4. 4 Correct answers unlock the next question envelope automatically and grant speed bonus points!
  5. 5 The first group to answer all 5 questions wins the Kahoot Grand Championship!
